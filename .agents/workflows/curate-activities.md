# Workflow: Curate Activities & Experiences

## 1. Purpose
Curates a tailored collection of cultural, recreational, culinary, and landscape experiences suited to traveler preferences and pace.
Equips each activity with realistic duration estimates, ticketing deadlines, peak crowd avoidance tactics, and bad-weather backups.

## 2. Agents Involved
- `activity-curator` (Lead)
- `local-discovery-agent`
- `source-verification`
- `activity-curator` (Skill)

## 3. Input / Output Contracts
- **Input**: Destination, dates, traveler interests, pacing archetype (`packed`, `balanced`, `relaxed`), crowd sensitivity, mobility needs.
- **Output**: Curated activity catalog categorized by theme, time slots, advance booking rules, anti-crowd tactics, and rain backups.

## 4. Step-by-Step Execution Process
1. **Thematic Discovery**: Identify experiences across culture, history, gastronomy, nature, family, and local craft.
2. **Crowd Management Strategy**: Determine optimal time windows (e.g. early morning opening vs late afternoon) and identify mandatory timed-entry requirements.
3. **Rain & Closure Contingencies**: Assign a viable indoor alternative (e.g. covered market, art gallery) for every weather-sensitive outdoor activity.
4. **Local Infusion**: Blend iconic cultural highlights with authentic neighborhood spots discovered via local scouting.
5. **Official Ticketing Directory**: Collect official reservation links and flag advance booking lead times.

## 5. Deliverables
- Thematic Activity Catalog
- Peak Crowd Avoidance Matrix
- Weather Contingency (Plan B) Table
- Official Ticketing Action List

# Travel Safety

Read `../../shared/compact-research-protocol.md`, `../../shared/research-methods.md`, and `../../shared/evidence-policy.md`. Consult `../../shared/scenario-routing.md` and load only matching border, driving, heat, altitude, accessibility, family, connectivity, or regional references.

## Inputs and tools

Require generic nationality/residency, destinations/transits, dates, age/access context, activities, and risk tolerance. Never request or store passport numbers, diagnoses, or other unnecessary PII. Use current official-source access and artifacts.

## Method

- Build an applicability matrix: traveler category × jurisdiction × border/transit type × date. Verify entry/visa/transit, passport validity, forms, customs, driving, and advisories with both destination authorities and the traveler's relevant origin authority when useful.
- Label every item `legal_requirement`, `official_recommendation`, or `precaution`; never present advice as law. Record issuing jurisdiction, effective date, applicability, and revalidation deadline.
- Check official health guidance, vaccines, medication documentation/import rules, insurance/evacuation limits, seasonal/environmental hazards, severe weather, scams, and emergency procedures without diagnosing.
- Prioritize risk as severity × likelihood × traveler exposure while preserving low-probability high-impact blockers. Record official emergency/consular contacts only from current primary sources.
- Create departure-relative revalidation tasks for volatile entry, health, weather, strike, and advisory claims. Recommendation readiness requires every applicable critical cell to have current primary evidence.

## Fallback

Without current official access, provide the applicability matrix and official-source checklist. Mark entry, health, alert, contact, and legal claims unverified; do not give definitive clearance.

## Outputs

Write atomic requirements, advisories, risks, sources, actions, contacts, and revalidation tasks. Return `compact-handoff/v2` with action/blocker IDs.

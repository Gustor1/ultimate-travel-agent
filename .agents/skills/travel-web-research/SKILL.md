# Travel Web Research

Read `../../shared/compact-research-protocol.md`, `../../shared/research-methods.md`, and `../../shared/evidence-policy.md`. Consult `../../shared/scenario-routing.md` and load only matching regional, family, connectivity, sustainability, heat, altitude, or accessibility references.

## Inputs and tools

Require destination/region, dates, non-PII traveler needs, interests, pace, crowd tolerance, access needs, and open decisions. Use current-source access, artifacts, multilingual queries, and deterministic calculations.

## Method

Build a topic × period × area × traveler-constraint × source-role ledger, then:

1. Establish official geography, districts, realistic scope, and transport anchors without duplicating operator schedules.
2. Research climate normals, daylight, seasonal hazards, and date-specific official outlooks separately.
3. Research holidays, major events, closures, and crowd drivers for the actual dates.
4. Identify cultural/historical and outdoor context through responsible institutions while leaving venue hours/tickets to activity ownership.
5. Establish customs, etiquette, practical norms, and useful local-language terms; distinguish durable context from rules.
6. Add recognized editorial context and alternative areas, then community/social leads only for qualitative discovery.
7. Use user-language, English, local-language, local-name, and transliteration query variants when useful. Resolve duplicate entities and sources.
8. Verify retained contextual and logistical claims on primary pages; record contradictions, effective dates, independence, and freshness.

Do not re-research entry/health advisories, exact venue logistics, property policies, or transport fares owned by another specialist. Publish resolved-question and source-demand IDs for reuse. Completion requires the full ledger at `pending: 0`; evidence sufficiency additionally requires each material topic to have an appropriate authoritative source or an explicit gap.

## Fallback

Without current-source access, use supplied evidence and return every pending claim/query. Keep events, closures, advisories, schedules, and current rules unverified.

## Outputs

Write the coverage ledger, atomic context claims, sources, resolved questions, source demands, conflicts, and rejection codes. Return `compact-handoff/v2` with IDs and gates.

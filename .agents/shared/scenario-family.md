# Family and minors scenario

Trigger only when minors travel or the user declares family-specific constraints. Use non-identifying age bands; never request dates of birth or document numbers.

- Safety owns consent, entry, document, health, and legal rules; verify them by nationality, relationship, jurisdiction, carrier, and date.
- Transport verifies infant/child fares, seating-together policy, restraints, stroller handling, baggage, transfers, and age eligibility. Never assume adjacent seats or through handling.
- Accommodation compares the exact declared occupancy and verifies cot, extra-bed, connecting-room, pool/child access, and supervision rules. A room label does not prove legal occupancy.
- Activities verify age/height rules, child pricing, accessibility, toilets, duration, and adult supervision.
- Itinerary and budget preserve meal/nap/rest windows, slower transfers, atomic child/adult costs, and a compatible fallback. Do not invent a universal family pace.

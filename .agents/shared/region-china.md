# China conditional grounding

Load this file only for mainland China or Hong Kong/mainland connections. Reverify all date-sensitive rules on official sources.

## Entry and borders

- Never assume a tourist visa. Verify nationality, stay length, dates, eligible ports/regions, and whether a landside or cross-border transfer qualifies under the current policy.
- Verify the currently effective visa-free-transit duration, eligible ports/regions, movement area, and conditions with the National Immigration Administration (`https://en.nia.gov.cn`) and the relevant visa centre (`https://www.visaforchina.cn`). Treat legacy 72/144-hour wording as stale until confirmed.
- Never put names, passport numbers, birth dates, or identity details in queries.

## Rail

- Present China Railway 12306 (`https://www.12306.cn/en/index.html` or its official app) as the direct operator and Trip.com (`https://www.trip.com/trains/china/`) as a practical international channel when appropriate.
- Compare current service fees, payment support, real-name/passport verification, account/SMS friction, sales hours, refund rules, and ticket delivery. Do not reuse old fee or refund percentages without verification.

## Accommodation

- Verify that each property accepts foreign guests and can perform the required local Public Security Bureau registration. Record `confirmed`, `unconfirmed`, or `to_verify`; never infer acceptance from price or domestic listings.

## Activities

- For the Beijing Palace Museum / Forbidden City, verify the current release window, real-name requirements, entry rules, and official ticket channel on `https://bookingticket.dpm.org.cn/` or `https://www.dpm.org.cn/`.
- Jingshan Park may be researched as a viewpoint alternative, with current official evidence.

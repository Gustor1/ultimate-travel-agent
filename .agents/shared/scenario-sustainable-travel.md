# Sustainable-travel scenario

Trigger only for an explicit environmental or community-impact preference. Treat it as a decision dimension, never as permission to override budget, safety, accessibility, or time constraints.

- Transport compares viable modes using one disclosed emissions methodology and compatible system boundaries. Keep emissions, duration, price, and robustness separate; ranges are preferable to false precision.
- Flight comparisons preserve aircraft/route assumptions when available and never treat offsets as zero emissions. Accommodation claims require a named certification, issuer, scope, and current validity rather than generic “eco” language.
- Destination/local research may flag congestion, protected-area rules, resident impact, local ownership, and seasonal pressure using responsible institutions and current policies.
- Record methodology/source IDs, uncertainty, excluded effects, and user trade-offs. Green claims without inspectable criteria remain unverified and cannot decide the recommendation alone.

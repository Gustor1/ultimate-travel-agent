# London conditional grounding

Load this file only for London research. Treat every fare, policy, opening rule, and fee below as a verification target, not timeless truth.

## Entry

- For non-UK travelers verify on gov.uk the accepted identity document for the traveler's nationality (EU national identity cards are generally no longer accepted) and whether an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) is required, its fee, and processing time. Starting point: `https://www.gov.uk/guidance/apply-for-an-electronic-travel-authorisation-eta`.

## Getting there

- From France, Belgium, or the Netherlands compare Eurostar against air door-to-door: station versus airport access, check-in cutoff, luggage allowance, and arrival terminus (St Pancras) versus airport transfer cost and time. Verify fares on `https://www.eurostar.com/` for the exact dates.

## Transport

- Compare direct airport operators appropriate to the arrival airport, including TfL Elizabeth/Piccadilly services, Heathrow Express, and Thameslink where applicable. Use exact operator pages and current travel dates.
- Compare contactless and Oyster using current TfL fares/caps, traveler stay length, bank foreign-transaction fees, device/card availability, and child/concession eligibility. Verify the Oyster issuance fee and all caps before stating them. TfL and National Rail fares are usually revised each March: a cap or fare observed before a March change does not apply to travel after it, so mark it `estimation` and add a revalidation task after the change date.
- Official starting points: `https://tfl.gov.uk/fares/`, `https://tfl.gov.uk/modes/elizabeth-line/`, `https://tfl.gov.uk/modes/tube/`, `https://www.heathrowexpress.com/`, `https://www.thameslinkrailway.com/`.

## Activities

- Distinguish free permanent collections from paid temporary exhibitions at the British Museum and National Gallery; verify timed-entry advice and exhibition prices on their direct visit pages.
- Tower of London tickets must use Historic Royal Palaces: `https://www.hrp.org.uk/tower-of-london/visit/tickets-and-prices/`.
- Time-sensitive bookings to place in the booking calendar: West End shows, Warner Bros. Studio Tour, free timed slots that open on a release date (for example Sky Garden), and popular restaurants. Verify release rules on each official page.
- Plan rain alternatives near outdoor anchors: London weather changes within the day, so pair parks, markets, and walks with a nearby museum or covered market.

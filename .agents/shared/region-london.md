# London conditional grounding

Load this file only for London research. Treat every fare, policy, opening rule, and fee below as a verification target, not timeless truth.

## Transport

- Compare direct airport operators appropriate to the arrival airport, including TfL Elizabeth/Piccadilly services, Heathrow Express, and Thameslink where applicable. Use exact operator pages and current travel dates.
- Compare contactless and Oyster using current TfL fares/caps, traveler stay length, bank foreign-transaction fees, device/card availability, and child/concession eligibility. Verify the Oyster issuance fee and all caps before stating them.
- Official starting points: `https://tfl.gov.uk/fares/`, `https://tfl.gov.uk/modes/elizabeth-line/`, `https://tfl.gov.uk/modes/tube/`, `https://www.heathrowexpress.com/`, `https://www.thameslinkrailway.com/`.

## Activities

- Distinguish free permanent collections from paid temporary exhibitions at the British Museum and National Gallery; verify timed-entry advice and exhibition prices on their direct visit pages.
- Tower of London tickets must use Historic Royal Palaces: `https://www.hrp.org.uk/tower-of-london/visit/tickets-and-prices/`.

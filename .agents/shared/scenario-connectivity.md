# Connectivity and remote-work scenario

Trigger for international connectivity decisions, multi-country roaming, remote work, or travel whose tickets/maps/contacts must work offline.

- Compare roaming, local SIM, and eSIM on device compatibility, unlock/eSIM requirement, countries, network, allowance, tethering, activation, validity, renewal, final price, and official coverage. Never request IMEI, phone number, account credentials, or activation secrets.
- Safety owns legal restrictions, emergency channels, censorship or service limitations; destination research owns general availability; accommodation owns property workspace/connectivity claims.
- A provider or hotel speed claim is not proof of stable remote-work performance. Preserve measurement date/method and require a recent independent signal or mark reliability unverified.
- Produce an offline pack obligation for critical tickets, maps, addresses, translations, emergency contacts, and recovery instructions. Avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive actions and never store session material.

# Dining scenario

Trigger when food is a trip objective, a dietary constraint affects feasibility, or a meal reservation anchors the schedule.

- Local discovery owns ordinary venue candidates; activity curation owns ticketed culinary experiences; itinerary owns meal windows and routing.
- Verify the exact branch, service days/hours, reservation channel, deposit/cancellation terms, current menu or price basis, accessibility, and dress/age rules from direct sources when operationally important.
- Treat reviews and community sources as qualitative evidence for atmosphere, noise, consistency, or local relevance. They do not verify hours, allergens, availability, or price.
- Record dietary statements and allergen policies exactly. Never convert “options available” into a safety guarantee; cross-contamination and medical suitability remain user-confirmed.
- Retain nearby compatible alternatives for fixed reservations, late arrivals, and closed kitchens without duplicating full venue evidence in the handoff.

# Loyalty and award scenario

Trigger only when the user explicitly wants points, miles, status benefits, certificates, or transfer partners considered. Never request account credentials, membership numbers, or balances; accept user-supplied non-sensitive totals and programme names.

- Flight/accommodation research compares cash and award options for identical dates, party, cabin/room, and cancellation class. Award availability is live evidence and expires within 24 hours.
- Verify the operating programme, saver/dynamic level, mixed cabin, taxes/surcharges, transfer ratio and delay, expiry, change/cancellation, status eligibility, and direct redemption channel.
- Budget records points and cash separately, including foregone value and irreversible-transfer exposure. Do not apply a universal cents-per-point value; use the user's stated valuation or show break-even ranges.
- Never recommend transferring points speculatively when inventory is not held or current. Preserve a cash fallback and the exact revalidation action.

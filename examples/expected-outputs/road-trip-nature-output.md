# Travel Dossier: Iceland South & West Ring Road (7 Days)

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Destination**: Iceland (South Coast & Snæfellsnes)
- **Duration**: 7 days / 6 nights in September
- **Party**: 2 adults (adventure travelers)
- **Estimated Total Cost**: 365,000 ISK + 55,000 ISK (15% reserve) = **420,000 ISK** (~€2,800 EUR)
- **Vehicle**: 4x4 SUV (Toyota RAV4 or similar) with Sand & Ash + Gravel Protection

## 2. Day-by-Day Route Overview
```yaml
day_1: "KEF Airport arrival, pick up 4x4, Blue Lagoon or Reykjanes Peninsula, drive to Selfoss (Overnight Selfoss)."
day_2: "Golden Circle: Þingvellir, Geysir, Gullfoss waterfall, Secret Lagoon (Overnight Hella)."
day_3: "South Coast: Seljalandsfoss, Gljúfrabúi, Skógafoss, Dyrhólaey puffin cliffs, Reynisfjara (Overnight Vík)."
day_4: "Skaftafell glacier hike (guided), Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon, Diamond Beach (Overnight Kirkjubæjarklaustur)."
day_5: "Fjaðrárgljúfur canyon, drive to West Iceland via Borgarfjörður (Overnight Borgarnes)."
day_6: "Snæfellsnes Peninsula: Kirkjufell peak, Djúpalónssandur black pebble beach, Arnarstapi cliffs (Overnight Grundarfjörður)."
day_7: "Drive to KEF via Hvalfjörður tunnel, return rental vehicle, departure flight."
```

## 3. Pre-Departure Safety Checklist & Live Links
- **Road Conditions**: Check daily morning updates on `https://www.road.is` (Vegagerðin).
- **Weather & Storm Warnings**: Check `https://safetravel.is` and `https://en.vedur.is`.
- **Aurora Activity**: Monitor Kp-index and cloud cover map on Vedur.is.

## 2. Airport & Local Transport Plan
- **Airport Transfers from Heathrow (LHR)**:
  - **Option 1 (Recommended)**: Elizabeth line direct from Heathrow Terminals 2/3 to Tottenham Court Road / Farringdon (35-40 min, £13.30 peak / £12.20 off-peak pay-as-you-go). Operator: TfL. Link: `https://tfl.gov.uk/modes/elizabeth-line/` (Verified 2026-09-16).
  - **Option 2 (Budget Alternative)**: Piccadilly line underground to Central London (55 min, £5.60 peak / £3.40 off-peak). Operator: TfL. Link: `https://tfl.gov.uk/modes/tube/` (Verified 2026-09-16).
  - **Option 3 (Express Alternative)**: Heathrow Express non-stop to Paddington (15 min, £25 single unless booked 90 days in advance). Operator: Heathrow Express. Link: `https://www.heathrowexpress.com/` (Verified 2026-09-16).
- **Underground & Bus: Contactless vs Oyster Comparative Analysis**:
  - **Payment Recommendation**: **Contactless Payment (Bank Card / Apple Pay / Google Pay) is strongly recommended for adult travelers**.
  - **Why**: Contactless uses the exact same pay-as-you-go fares and automatic daily/weekly caps as an Oyster card (Zone 1-2 daily cap £8.50 as of 2026-09-16), but avoids the £7 non-refundable Oyster card issuance fee and the inconvenience of queuing to top up credit.
  - **When Oyster is Preferred**: Travelers whose bank charges high international transaction fees per swipe, or families traveling with children aged 11-15 who require the Young Visitor Discount (which must be applied by TfL staff onto a physical Oyster card at a station).
  - **Official TfL Fares Comparison Portal**: `https://tfl.gov.uk/fares/how-to-pay-and-save/pay-as-you-go/contactless-and-oyster-compared` (Tier 1) and `https://tfl.gov.uk/fares/` (Tier 1).
- **Verification Date**: 2026-09-16

## 4. Curated Activities: Free Galleries vs Paid Monuments
1. **The British Museum (Free Permanent Collection)**
   - **Location**: Great Russell Street, Bloomsbury, London
   - **Verified Price**: **FREE general admission** to permanent galleries (Rosetta Stone, Parthenon Sculptures). Special temporary exhibitions are ticketed (£18-£24).
   - **Booking Policy**: Pre-booking a free timed entry slot is recommended to skip the general standby queue.
   - **Direct Official URL**: `https://www.britishmuseum.org/visit` (Tier 1)
   - **Verification Date**: 2026-09-16

2. **The National Gallery (Free Permanent Collection)**
   - **Location**: Trafalgar Square, London
   - **Verified Price**: **FREE general admission** to permanent collection (Van Gogh, Turner, Da Vinci). Temporary feature exhibits ticketed.
   - **Booking Policy**: Walk-in allowed, advance free entry reservation recommended.
   - **Direct Official URL**: `https://www.nationalgallery.org.uk/visiting` (Tier 1)
   - **Verification Date**: 2026-09-16

3. **Tower of London (Paid Monument)**
   - **Location**: Tower Hill, London EC3N 4AB
   - **Verified Price**: £34.80 adult with donation / £31.60 standard entry (Verified 2026-09-16)
   - **Booking Policy**: Timed admission ticket booked in advance. Avoid third-party markup blogs.
   - **Direct Official Booking URL**: Historic Royal Palaces (HRP) official ticketing — `https://www.hrp.org.uk/tower-of-london/visit/tickets-and-prices/` (Tier 1)

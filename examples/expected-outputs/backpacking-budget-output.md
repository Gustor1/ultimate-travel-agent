# Travel Dossier: Vietnam Central Backpacking Route (10 Days)

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Destination**: Vietnam (Hanoi, Ninh Binh, Phong Nha, Hoi An)
- **Duration**: 10 days / 9 nights
- **Party**: 1 solo backpacker
- **Estimated Budget**: 13,000,000 VND + 2,000,000 VND (safety reserve) = **15,000,000 VND** (~$600 USD)
- **Transport**: DSVN Overnight sleeper trains (SE1 / SE3) + cycling

## 2. Route Schedule & Overnight Logistics
- **Day 1-2**: Hanoi Old Quarter street food, Train Street (viewed from safe cafe), West Lake.
- **Day 3**: Ninh Binh / Trang An boat rowers and Mua Cave sunrise viewpoint.
- **Day 4-5**: Sleeper train to Dong Hoi; explore Phong Nha and Paradise Cave.
- **Day 6-9**: Train to Da Nang, bus to Hoi An Ancient Town; tailor shops, An Bang beach.
- **Day 10**: Da Nang airport departure.

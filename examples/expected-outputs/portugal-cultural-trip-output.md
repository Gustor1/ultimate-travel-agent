## 2. Entry Formalities & Travel Safety
- **Entry Regulations**:
  - **Schengen Area**: Portugal is part of the Schengen zone. EU citizens travel with valid national ID card or passport. Non-EU visa-exempt travelers (e.g. UK, US, Canada) enter for up to 90 days in any 180-day period.
  - **Administrative Authority**: **AIMA (Agência para a Integração, Migrações e Asilo)** is the sole active immigration and asylum agency in Portugal. **The former SEF (Serviço de Estrangeiros e Fronteiras) was permanently dissolved in October 2023** and must not be referenced as an active authority.
  - **Official Entry Portals**:
    - AIMA Official Portal — Tier 1: `https://aima.gov.pt` (Verified 2026-09-16)
    - Portuguese Ministry of Foreign Affairs Consular / Visa Portal — Tier 1: `https://vistos.mne.gov.pt/en/` (Verified 2026-09-16)
- **Verification Date**: 2026-09-16

## 3. Intercity Transport & Electronic Tolls Plan
- **Intercity Train (Lisbon -> Porto)**:
  - **Route**: Lisbon Santa Apolónia / Oriente to Porto Campanhã
  - **Operator**: CP (Comboios de Portugal)
  - **Service**: Alfa Pendular (AP, 2h50m) or Intercidades (IC, 3h15m)
  - **Official Ticketing Portal**: `https://www.cp.pt/` (Tier 2) / Promo Tickets: `https://www.cp.pt/passageiros/en/discounts-tickets/discounts/promo-tickets` (Tier 2)
  - **Estimated Fare**: €15.50 - €25.00 per person in 2nd class (Turística) when booked 5 to 60 days ahead with "Bilhete Promo" (up to 65% discount). Regular full fare is €33.50 (Verified 2026-09-16).
- **Electronic Tolls (Portagens Eletrónicas) Guidance**:
  - **System Overview**: Several Portuguese motorways (such as ex-SCUT routes A22 in Algarve, A28, A4 near Porto) use **100% electronic toll gantries without physical toll booths or cash lanes**.
  - **For Foreign-Registered Vehicles**: Drivers must register in advance on the official portal using **EasyToll** (which associates the vehicle license plate with a bank card for 30 days, +€0.32 administrative fee per trip) or purchase a pre-paid **TollCard**: `https://www.portugaltolls.com/en/tolls-payment` (Tier 1, Verified 2026-09-16).
  - **For Car Rentals in Portugal**: Rent the **Via Verde** electronic transponder directly from the car rental company (typically €1.50 - €2.00/day). Tolls will automatically be charged to the credit card upon return.
- **Verification Date**: 2026-09-16

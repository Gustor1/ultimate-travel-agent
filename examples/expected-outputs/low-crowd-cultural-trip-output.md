# Travel Dossier: Umbria Quiet Hill Towns (5 Days)

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Destination**: Umbria, Italy
- **Duration**: 5 days / 4 nights
- **Party**: 2 adults
- **Estimated Cost**: €1,550 EUR + €250 EUR reserve = **€1,800 EUR**
- **Pacing**: Relaxed (1 hill town per day, zero rushing, agriturismo base)

## 2. Highlights & ZTL Parking Guidance
- **Orvieto**: St. Patrick's Well and Duomo; park at Campo della Fiera (underground elevator access).
- **Assisi**: Early morning 08:30 visit to Basilica of San Francesco before tour buses arrive.
- **Montefalco**: Sagrantino wine tasting and olive oil harvest visit.
- **Spoleto**: Ponte delle Torri and Roman theatre.

# Travel Dossier: Brittany & Normandy Family Discovery (6 Days)

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Destination**: Normandy & Northern Brittany, France
- **Duration**: 6 days / 5 nights
- **Party**: 4 travelers (2 adults, 2 kids aged 7 & 10)
- **Estimated Total Cost**: €1,920 EUR + €280 EUR reserve = **€2,200 EUR**
- **Focus**: Medieval walls, Mont Saint-Michel, crêperies, pirate folklore

## 2. Key Highlights & Child-Friendly Pacing
- **Saint-Malo**: Walk the granite ramparts at low tide; visit Fort National on foot across the sands.
- **Mont Saint-Michel**: Scheduled late afternoon arrival (after 15:30) to avoid morning bus congestion.
- **Dinan**: Cobblestone descent of Rue du Jerzual and river boat rental on the Rance.
- **Rain Plan B**: Grand Aquarium de Saint-Malo & Cité de la Voile Éric Tabarly.

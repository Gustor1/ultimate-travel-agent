# Scenario: Backpacking Budget (Vietnam Central Route, 10 Days)

## 1. User Brief
- **Destination**: Vietnam (Hanoi, Ninh Binh, Phong Nha caves, Hoi An)
- **Duration**: 10 days / 9 nights in November
- **Origin**: Bangkok (BKK) to Hanoi (HAN)
- **Travelers**: 1 solo backpacker (young adult)
- **Budget & Currency**: 15,000,000 VND (~$600 USD) excluding international flights
- **Pace Preference**: `packed` (active exploring, night trains to save on lodging, caving, bicycle riding)
- **Crowd Preference**: `low-crowd` (early morning cycling through Tam Coc, independent cave hikes)
- **Transport Preference**: Overnight sleeper trains (Vietnam Railways), regional buses, rental scooter / bicycle
- **Interests**: Karst landscapes, cave exploration, street food culture, historic silk towns, coffee culture
- **Dietary**: Adventurous street food eater; no allergies

## 2. Skills Mobilized
`travel-orchestrator`, `transport-research`, `travel-web-research`, `accommodation-research`, `local-discovery`, `budget-and-booking-checker`, `travel-safety`, `itinerary-optimizer`, `quality-controller`

## 3. Sub-Agents to Launch
All 11 sub-agents with emphasis on transport efficiency and safety verification.

## 4. Expected Sources to Consult
- Vietnam National Administration of Tourism — Tier 1 (`https://vietnam.travel`)
- Vietnam Railways (DSVN Official) — Tier 2 (`https://dsvn.vn`)
- Phong Nha-Ke Bang National Park Official Guide — Tier 1
- National Centre for Hydro-Meteorological Forecasting (NCHMF Vietnam) — Tier 1

## 5. Data Requiring Verification Before Booking
1. **Vietnam e-Visa Validity**: Apply strictly via official portal (`https://evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn`) at least 2 weeks prior.
2. **Central Vietnam Rainy Season**: November can experience monsoon rains and flooding in Hoi An; check river water levels.
3. **Sleeper Train Berth Reservation**: Book lower soft sleeper berths on official DSVN portal to avoid reseller surcharges.
4. **Scooter Driving Legality**: 1968 International Driving Permit required; helmet mandatory.

## 6. Identified Risks & Mitigations
- Tap water is non-potable; drink boiled or bottled water exclusively.
- Flooding in Hoi An ancient town in late autumn; keep flexible hotel cancellation in Da Nang as Plan B.

## 7. Expected Deliverable Format
Tight budget breakdown by day ($45/day average + 15% emergency reserve), sleeper train schedule, vetted social hostel shortlist, and street food etiquette guide.

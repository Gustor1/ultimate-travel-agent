# Scenario: Family Trip (Brittany & Normandy, 6 Days)

## 1. User Brief
- **Destination**: Normandy & Northern Brittany, France (Saint-Malo, Dinan, Mont Saint-Michel, D-Day beaches)
- **Duration**: 6 days / 5 nights during spring school break (late April)
- **Origin**: Paris (Montparnasse TGV to Rennes, then rental car)
- **Travelers**: 4 people (2 adults, 2 children aged 7 and 10)
- **Budget & Currency**: €2,200 EUR total (excluding Paris-Rennes TGV)
- **Pace Preference**: `relaxed` (morning activity, child-friendly lunch, afternoon discovery or beach walk, early bedtime)
- **Crowd Preference**: `mixed` (visit Mont Saint-Michel late afternoon when day-tripper buses depart)
- **Interests**: Coastal pirate ramparts, medieval castles, interactive history, crêperies, tidal walks
- **Dietary & Needs**: Children love crêpes and galettes; need family suite or 2-bedroom rental with kitchen

## 2. Skills Mobilized
`travel-orchestrator`, `activity-curator`, `transport-research`, `accommodation-research`, `budget-and-booking-checker`, `travel-safety`, `itinerary-optimizer`, `quality-controller`

## 3. Sub-Agents to Launch
All 11 sub-agents tailored for family dynamics and child pacing.

## 4. Expected Sources to Consult
- Tourisme Bretagne (Official Regional Board) — Tier 1 (`https://www.brittanytourism.com`)
- Centre des Monuments Nationaux (Abbaye du Mont-Saint-Michel) — Tier 1 (`https://www.abbaye-mont-saint-michel.fr`)
- SNCF Connect — Tier 2
- Météo-France — Tier 1

## 5. Data Requiring Verification Before Booking
1. **Mont Saint-Michel Tidal Coefficients**: Check high tide times; spring tides can submerge access ramps.
2. **Abbaye St-Michel Family Tickets**: Children under 18 enter free with EU citizenship; online time slot reservation remains mandatory.
3. **Child Car Seat Requirements**: French law requires child safety seats up to 10 years / 135 cm.
4. **Rampart Stroller Accessibility**: Saint-Malo ramparts feature steep stone staircases; lightweight carrier advised over bulky strollers.

## 6. Identified Risks & Mitigations
- Bay of Mont-Saint-Michel quicksands: Never walk into the bay without a certified professional tidal guide.
- Weather variability: Cool coastal sea breezes; pack warm windbreakers and rainboots.

## 7. Expected Deliverable Format
Family-friendly chronological schedule with dedicated snack/rest windows, tide charts, interactive castle visits, and family guesthouse shortlist.

# Scenario: Portugal Heritage & Coast (Lisbon, Sintra & Porto, 7 Days)

## 1. User Brief
- **Destination**: Lisbon, Sintra, and Porto, Portugal
- **Duration**: 7 days / 6 nights (May 10 - May 17)
- **Origin**: Paris Orly (direct flight to Lisbon LIS, return from Porto OPO)
- **Travelers**: 2 adults (couple)
- **Budget & Currency**: €2,200 EUR
- **Pace Preference**: `balanced` (morning monument, scenic lunch, afternoon walking/coastal discovery)
- **Interests**: Manueline & Romanticist architecture, authentic Fado, Douro wine, scenic rail
- **Transport Preference**: CP high-speed/intercity train Lisbon-Porto, rental car or public transit for Sintra/Cascais, clear electronic toll explanation

## 2. Skills Mobilized
`travel-orchestrator`, `travel-safety`, `transport-research`, `accommodation-research`, `activity-curator`, `local-discovery`, `budget-and-booking-checker`, `itinerary-builder`, `source-verification`, `travel-quality-control`

## 3. Required Grounding & Sourcing Invariants
- **Entry Policy**: Correctly reference AIMA (aima.gov.pt) and consular portal (vistos.mne.gov.pt); strictly avoid presenting defunct SEF as active.
- **Intercity Trains**: CP Comboios de Portugal promo tickets (cp.pt).
- **Electronic Tolls**: Motorways without toll booths (former SCUT); EasyToll and Via Verde rules (portugaltolls.com).
- **Sintra Ticket**: Parques de Sintra official ticketing for Pena Palace (bilheteira.parquesdesintra.pt).
- **Accommodation**: Validated Portuguese tourism licensing (RNET / Alojamento Local on rnt.turismodeportugal.pt).

# Scenario: Low-Crowd Cultural Trip (Umbria, Italy, 5 Days)

## 1. User Brief
- **Destination**: Umbria, Italy (Perugia, Assisi, Spoleto, Orvieto, Montefalco)
- **Duration**: 5 days / 4 nights in late October
- **Origin**: Rome Fiumicino (FCO), direct train to Orvieto/Perugia
- **Travelers**: 2 adults (mature cultural travelers)
- **Budget & Currency**: €1,800 EUR total
- **Pace Preference**: `relaxed` (1 hill town per day, unhurried art and wine appreciation)
- **Crowd Preference**: `low-crowd` (early morning basilicas, quiet countryside agriturismi, olive harvest season)
- **Interests**: Renaissance frescoes (Giotto, Perugino), medieval architecture, Sagrantino wine, olive oil mills
- **Things to Avoid**: Tour bus midday arrival times; driving into ZTL (restricted traffic zones)
- **Transport Preference**: Regional Trenitalia rail between towns, local taxi or short rental car for countryside

## 2. Skills Mobilized
`travel-orchestrator`, `travel-web-research`, `transport-research`, `accommodation-research`, `activity-curator`, `local-discovery`, `budget-and-booking-checker`, `travel-safety`, `itinerary-optimizer`, `quality-controller`

## 3. Expected Sources to Consult
- Umbria Tourism (Regione Umbria Official) — Tier 1 (`https://www.umbriatourism.it`)
- Trenitalia — Tier 2 (`https://www.trenitalia.com`)
- Basilica Papale di San Francesco in Assisi — Tier 1 (`https://www.sanfrancescoassisi.org`)
- Ministero della Cultura (MiC Italia) — Tier 1

## 4. Data Requiring Verification Before Booking
1. **Italian ZTL (Zona a Traffico Limitato)**: Strict automated camera enforcement with hefty fines; check parking garages outside historic gates.
2. **Basilica of Saint Francis Dress Code**: Shoulders and knees must be covered; photography strictly prohibited inside Lower Church.
3. **Olive Harvest Frantoi Open Days**: Late October features 'Frantoi Aperti'; verify participating olive mills.
4. **Regional Train Ticket Validation**: Paper tickets must be stamped in green/yellow validation machines prior to boarding.

## 5. Expected Deliverable Format
Quiet cultural itinerary with hill town walking routes, agriturismo farmstay shortlist, wine tasting booking links, and ZTL parking guides.

# Scenario: Business Trip with Extension (London, 3 Days)

## 1. User Brief
- **Destination**: London, United Kingdom (City of London / Canary Wharf + West End evening)
- **Duration**: 3 days / 2 nights (Tuesday to Thursday)
- **Origin**: Brussels-Midi (Eurostar)
- **Travelers**: 1 professional traveler
- **Budget & Currency**: £1,100 GBP corporate travel budget
- **Pace Preference**: `packed` daytime (meetings), `balanced` evening (theatre and fine dining)
- **Transport Preference**: Eurostar business premier / standard premier, Elizabeth line, London Underground
- **Interests**: Efficient transit, quiet business-ready hotel with high-speed Wi-Fi, West End theatre show, historic pub dining
- **Dietary & Needs**: Need iron/ironing board, late check-in, reliable quiet workspace

## 2. Skills Mobilized
`travel-orchestrator`, `transport-research`, `accommodation-research`, `activity-curator`, `budget-and-booking-checker`, `travel-safety`, `itinerary-optimizer`, `quality-controller`

## 3. Expected Sources to Consult
- Transport for London (TfL) — Tier 1 (`https://tfl.gov.uk`)
- Eurostar Official Portal — Tier 2 (`https://www.eurostar.com`)
- Visit London Official Guide — Tier 1 (`https://www.visitlondon.com`)
- Society of London Theatre (Official London Theatre) — Tier 1 (`https://officiallondontheatre.com`)

## 4. Data Requiring Verification Before Booking
1. **UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA)**: Check traveler eligibility and registration requirement prior to Eurostar boarding.
2. **Elizabeth Line Heathrow / St Pancras Transfer Time**: Farringdon interchange enables 12-minute transit between St Pancras and City.
3. **Contactless Fare Capping**: Using contactless bank cards automatically applies daily TfL fare caps; no Oyster card purchase required.
4. **Theatre Box Office Seat Allocation**: Book directly through venue box office for real-time seat view previews.

## 5. Expected Deliverable Format
Precision corporate itinerary with minute-by-minute transit times, business hotel shortlist near Liverpool Street / Farringdon, and dinner reservations.

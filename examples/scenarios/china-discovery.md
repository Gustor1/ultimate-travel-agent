# Scenario: China Cultural Discovery (Beijing & Shanghai, 10 Days)

## 1. User Brief
- **Destination**: Beijing & Shanghai, China
- **Duration**: 10 days / 9 nights (October 10 - October 20)
- **Origin**: Paris CDG (Air France direct to Beijing Capital PEK, return from Shanghai Pudong PVG)
- **Travelers**: 2 adult travelers (French citizens, ordinary passports)
- **Budget & Currency**: €4,500 EUR (~34,500 CNY)
- **Pace Preference**: `balanced` (1 major monument morning, cultural district afternoon, evenings open)
- **Interests**: Imperial history, modern high-speed rail, regional culinary traditions, traditional hutongs
- **Dietary & Needs**: Non-smoker rooms, preference for verified international foreign guest registration

## 2. Skills Mobilized
`travel-orchestrator`, `travel-safety`, `transport-research`, `accommodation-research`, `activity-curator`, `local-discovery`, `itinerary-builder`, `budget-and-booking-checker`, `source-verification`, `travel-quality-control`

## 3. Required Grounding & Sourcing Invariants
- **Entry Policy**: Must evaluate French citizenship under current unilateral visa-free exemption (en.nia.gov.cn) rather than automatically requiring a visa.
- **Intercity Rail**: High-speed G-train booked via China Railway official portal (12306.cn/en/index.html).
- **Accommodation**: Explicit verification of foreign guest registration (PSB / 涉外 acceptance).
- **Major Monuments**: Timed online booking for Forbidden City (bookingticket.dpm.org.cn).
- **Zero-PII Compliance**: No traveler names or passport details in external queries.

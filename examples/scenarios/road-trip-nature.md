# Scenario: Road Trip Nature (Iceland Ring Road, 7 Days)

## 1. User Brief
- **Destination**: South & West Iceland (Golden Circle, South Coast to Jökulsárlón, Snæfellsnes)
- **Duration**: 7 days / 6 nights in early September
- **Origin**: Frankfurt (FRA) to Keflavík (KEF)
- **Travelers**: 2 adults (adventure travelers)
- **Budget & Currency**: 420,000 ISK (~€2,800 EUR) excluding flights
- **Pace Preference**: `balanced` (driving max 3.5 to 4 hours per day, multiple scenic hikes)
- **Crowd Preference**: `low-crowd` (dawn stops at waterfalls, remote black sand viewpoints)
- **Interests**: Volcanic landscapes, glacial lagoons, waterfalls, geothermal baths, northern lights spotting
- **Transport Preference**: 4x4 SUV rental car with gravel and sand/ash insurance
- **Things to Avoid**: Dangerous cliff edges in high winds; driving on F-roads during river swells

## 2. Skills Mobilized
- `travel-orchestrator`, `transport-research`, `travel-web-research`, `accommodation-research`, `activity-curator`, `local-discovery`, `budget-and-booking-checker`, `travel-safety`, `itinerary-optimizer`, `quality-controller`

## 3. Sub-Agents to Launch
All 11 specialized sub-agents across 5 waves.

## 4. Expected Sources to Consult
- SafeTravel Iceland (Official Hazard & Road Alerts) — Tier 1 (`https://safetravel.is`)
- Vegagerðin (Icelandic Road and Coastal Administration) — Tier 1 (`https://www.road.is`)
- Vedur.is (Icelandic Meteorological Office & Aurora Forecast) — Tier 1 (`https://en.vedur.is`)
- Visit Iceland (National Tourism Board) — Tier 1 (`https://www.visiticeland.com`)

## 5. Data Requiring Verification Before Booking
1. **Gravel & Ash Rental Insurance**: Verify CDW and Super CDW zero-excess options.
2. **Reynisfjara Sneaker Waves**: Check daily danger warning lights (green/yellow/red) at the beach.
3. **Glacier Lagoon Boat Tours**: Zodiac and amphibian tours end for the winter in mid-September.
4. **Early Snow / Road Closures**: Review highland weather transition conditions on Vedur.is.

## 6. Identified Risks & Mitigations
- Rapid weather shifts (gale force winds, sudden horizontal rain); pack waterproof Gore-Tex layers.
- Car doors bending backwards in gale-force winds; hold doors firmly with both hands when opening.
- Long distances between petrol stations; refill whenever the tank drops below half.

## 7. Expected Deliverable Format
Day-by-day road trip driving plan with kilometer markers, driving buffers, safe parking coordinates, eco-cabin shortlist, and weather emergency links.
